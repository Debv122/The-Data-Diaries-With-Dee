Capstone: the backlog

You are dropped into one fictional new brand's genuinely bad week. Time: about two weeks, part time. Marked on judgement and on what you chose to leave undone — not on tidiness.

All at once, with more arriving than you can finish:

  1. Clear a stacked sixty-ticket backlog across three channels (email, live chat, WhatsApp), keeping a written judgement log of every call you made.
  2. De-escalate a public complaint thread that is gaining replies, and keep the customer.
  3. Resolve a policy grey area: the correct-by-the-letter answer and the fair answer are not the same thing. Decide, and write down why.
  4. Catch a chatbot that has been quietly promising a discount which does not exist, fix the ten AI-drafted replies affected, and explain the business risk of each.
  5. Rewrite three knowledge-base articles that caused confusion this week, and flag the underlying pattern upward to the founder.
  6. Write one honest weekly metrics report: the real numbers, and the reasons behind a bad one — not just the numbers.

Finish with a five-minute walkthrough of your week, recorded or written, marked on how you handle being challenged on a decision — not on how polished it sounds.

Do not include any real customer data, names or account details. Use made-up names throughout.
